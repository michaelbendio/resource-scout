# Provo classification pilot: proposed definitions

These are proposed definitions for the authorized historical Provo package, not approved office changes. No labels will be renamed or added. Existing unsupported assignments remain visible as unconfirmed.

## Categories

- **Addiction:** Treatment, recovery support, or sober housing directly addressing substance use.
- **Children/Pregnancy:** Direct pregnancy, child care, parenting, or child-development services. Serving a family alone is insufficient.
- **Clothing/Household:** Direct help obtaining clothing or essential household goods.
- **Disability:** Disability-related services that support daily living, access, independence, or participation. General availability to disabled people is insufficient.
- **Domestic Violence:** Direct safety planning, shelter, advocacy, or recovery support for domestic violence.
- **Education:** Instruction, literacy, educational credentials, or direct help accessing education. Financial literacy classes can qualify.
- **Employment:** Direct help finding, obtaining, or keeping work, including placement and employment preparation.
- **Financial Assistance:** Direct financial aid, benefits enrollment, or an accountable pathway to a named assistance program. Financial education alone belongs in Education.
- **Reentry Support:** Direct help with the practical transition from incarceration to community life. General availability to people with convictions is insufficient.
- **Food:** Direct meals, groceries, food benefits, or enrollment in a named food program.
- **Medical, Dental, Vision:** Direct medical, dental, or vision care or a named, accountable pathway to that care.
- **Homeless Services:** Direct assistance with shelter or essential daily needs while homeless, or an accountable housing-entry service. General referrals alone are insufficient.
- **Housing:** Direct help obtaining or keeping housing, including housing provision, subsidy, tenancy support, or enrollment in a named housing program.
- **ID Recovery:** Direct help obtaining or replacing identification or vital documents.
- **Legal:** Legal advice, representation, or a named legal-assistance intake pathway.
- **Mental Health:** Direct mental-health assessment, treatment, crisis response, or structured support.
- **Seniors:** Legacy population category for older adults. Retain existing memberships until a complete reviewed migration maps each resource to service categories and supported groups.
- **Transportation:** Direct rides, transportation assistance, or enrollment in a named transportation service. Merely being near transit is insufficient.
- **Utilities, Phone, Internet:** Direct help obtaining or keeping electricity, heating, water, phone, or internet service.
- **Veterans:** Legacy population category for veterans. Retain existing memberships until a complete reviewed migration maps each resource to service categories and supported groups.

## Groups

- **Exiting corrections:** People preparing to leave incarceration or returning to community life, supported by a dedicated service or a concrete accommodation. General eligibility is insufficient.
- **Families with children:** Households with children explicitly targeted by a service or meaningfully accommodated through a specific program. Serving adults who might be parents is insufficient.
- **Medically vulnerable:** **Leave pending.** The label does not tell us which health risks or accommodations qualify. Do not infer this group until the office clarifies it.
- **Seniors:** Older adults explicitly targeted or meaningfully accommodated by a program. Record the actual program age threshold; do not impose one age on every resource.
- **Spanish speaking:** People who can use a documented Spanish-language service or access arrangement. Website translation alone does not establish service in Spanish; language does not imply ethnicity.
- **Veterans:** Veterans explicitly targeted or meaningfully accommodated by a program. Include military family members or active-duty service members only when the specific program expressly includes them.
- **Women:** Women explicitly targeted or meaningfully accommodated by a specific service. General availability is insufficient.

## Types included in this pilot

- **Career (education):** Instruction or education planning aimed at preparing for an occupation. Job placement alone belongs in Employment.
- **Temp Agencies (employment):** A staffing agency directly placing applicants in temporary or contract jobs; it may also offer permanent placements.
- **Rent Assistance (financial-assistance):** Financial help paying rent or arrears through a named program.
- **Utilities assistance (financial-assistance):** Financial help paying household utility bills through a named program.
- **Meals (food):** Prepared meals provided directly to people who need food.
- **Pantries (food):** Groceries or food boxes distributed for use at home. Specific program eligibility remains in the resource information.

Other existing Types stay pending. Scout can preserve earlier assignments as unconfirmed and suggest a missing Type separately, but cannot invent or apply new Types.

The immediate review decision is whether to use these definitions for this pilot, leaving **Medically vulnerable** and the other Types pending. This does not approve resource classifications, production exports, taxonomy migrations, or learning activation.
