## Decisions to discuss

- Keep the older-Americans fact sheet and Justice in Aging available as clearly
  identified reference material. The fact sheet cannot honestly receive an
  existing service category, and Justice in Aging's training is for advocates.
  Their treatment needs a decision before the migration can pass its service
  category requirement.
- Confirm local knowledge behind Boulders/Seniors, One New Light/Disability,
  Provo Housing Authority/Addiction and DWS Overview/Housing. These existing human
  memberships remain unconfirmed; missing website statements did not erase them.
- Review the existing undefined Medically vulnerable group using MVP's concrete
  eligibility evidence. Review the pending Crisis & Support Type separately;
  catalog presence alone did not authorize applying it.
- Preserve referral questions about Oasis's Provo coverage, One New Light's
  current operation, the veteran outreach handoff, Utah Valley Rides' conflicting
  age thresholds, and Utah Housing funding/language availability.

The audits added verified MAG caregiver support groups, Vet Center counseling
for children, and named Utah Case Management transition assistance and classes.
The latter's general post-incarceration eligibility did not establish a dedicated
reentry service, so its new Reentry Support and Exiting corrections proposals
were withdrawn. Actual wheelchair ride access and Spanish application access
were assessed against the approved definitions, not a narrower meaning invented
by an auditor. No pending definition was approved and no lesson was activated.
