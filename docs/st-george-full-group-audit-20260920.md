All **879 resources** were reviewed individually against the **25 existing For-group
definitions**, including retained assignments and plausible missing assignments.
The resulting navigation revision changes **554 resources**: **1,006 assignments
added and 36 removed**. **782 resources** now have one or more groups; **97** have
explicit, recorded no-specific-group decisions. The 25 definitions and all Category

This completes the requested group-assignment audit of the displayed workbench.
It supersedes the earlier limited sensory-parent correction; that correction did
not establish semantic completeness for the rest of the records.

## What was reviewed

The review covered each final resource's name, description, complete saved
eligibility text, other saved population/service evidence, and the group definitions.
Supporting excerpts are recorded for every final assignment. Explicit no-group
decisions are recorded for all 97 ungrouped resources. Four questions were resolved
against official provider pages. A consistency pass checked similar programs and
removed provisional assignments that did not meet their definitions.

This is an audit of the final 879 resource records, not a fresh search of every
provider website, a replay of all historical research, or telephone verification.
It improves the finding aids without claiming that classifications or availability
are infallible. Human vetting remains the next stage.

## Decisions that matter for AND matching

- Every one of the **31 Deaf & hard of hearing** and **26 Blind & low vision**
  resources also has **People with disabilities**. The Children's Hearing Aid
  Program remains findable with the hearing and disability groups selected.
- Explicit child eligibility and child-serving pathways receive Children & teens
  and the applicable family label. Adult-only parenting courses and parent text
  tips do not receive a child-participation label merely because they discuss children.
  Childcare benefits, special-education advocacy and child health navigation still
  count as child-serving pathways even when a parent applies.
- Dedicated transition-age programs support Young adults; general adult eligibility
  does not. High-school seniors do not establish the Seniors group. Public learning
  alone does not establish Students, while enrolled school programs do.
- Free service alone does not establish Low-income households. Income rules,
  means-tested benefits and documented income-based discounts do. A child's benefit
  that excludes parental income is not automatically a low-income-household program.
- Paid caregiver jobs and professional provider training do not establish the
  family-carer support group. “Foster Grandparent” volunteer wording does not
  establish foster-family eligibility. A veteran sponsor's disability does not
  automatically make a dependent-only benefit a disability service for that sponsor.
- Documented Spanish access establishes a language finding aid; Latino or bilingual
  wording alone does not. Documented disability accommodations can justify a group
  even when the service is otherwise general, but do not guarantee clinical fit.
- Women, Men and identity-specific labels require an explicit pathway under their
  definitions. General access and a provider name alone are insufficient.

AND matches **resource labels**, not a person's eligibility. A broad resource may
contain several programs with different rules. Its inclusion in two groups does
not prove that one subprogram serves their intersection. Read the program-specific
Information and confirm fit. The four Information headings remain present on all
879 resources.

## Four source clarifications

Short source-supported notes were added under Access to explain the relevant
pathways. Existing text was preserved, including each stored category copy's own
wording; revisions and the pre-change database remain available.

1. **Switchpoint Community Resource Center:** family housing and a playground
   establish a child-family route. The note preserves uncertainty about placement
   and child emergency-bed availability. [Official campus page](https://switchpoint.org/where-we-are/locations/st-george/community-resource-center).
2. **Utah Tech Student Resource Center:** student-parent childcare funding, nursing
   room access and the intellectual-disability transition program are explicit.
   They are distinct from pantry access; each has its own rules. Saved SNAP/WIC
   application help also supports the income-benefit navigation label.
   [Official SRC page](https://src.utahtech.edu/).
3. **Suazo Business Center:** the provider explicitly lists Spanish-taught virtual
   business classes. Local advising language still needs confirmation. Conflicting
   local-hours wording remains visible rather than being silently resolved.
   [Official Spanish class page](https://suazocenter.org/es/tome-una-clase/).
4. **Tech-Moms:** its named online career-relaunch course establishes a women-specific
   option. Direct child participation and Spanish access in the announced Digital
   Literacy class were not counted as current access because an actual offering
   was not confirmed. Current childcare support still supports the family label.
   [Official programs page](https://www.tech-moms.org/tech-moms).
