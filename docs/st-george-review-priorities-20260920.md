## Judgment and limits

the saved records, service pathways, local access, eligibility, practical intake
routes and consequences of missing a service. Broad local entry points and urgent
or hard-to-replace specialized pathways receive early attention. The tiers were
not generated by a keyword scoring rule or a fixed numerical quota. Within each
tier, entries are alphabetical; that is not a further quality ranking.

Every membership has an explicit tier, reason and exact supporting excerpt in the
[decision evidence](st-george-review-priorities-evidence-20260920.json). The
[readable assignment table](st-george-review-priorities-20260920.csv) lists every
resource, Category, reason and question. Important specialized help is not a
judgment of lower importance: it makes targeted eligibility or needs visible.
Additional options remain available for alternatives, preference and capacity.

This assessment uses the already-reviewed record set. It does not establish current
vacancies, enrollment, funding, service quality or phone-verified eligibility.
Questions deliberately leave such uncertainty for human vetting. Matt's local
knowledge can improve this initial order. No resource was marked human Curated or
group-confirmed, and no new research, curation or paid review worker was launched.
