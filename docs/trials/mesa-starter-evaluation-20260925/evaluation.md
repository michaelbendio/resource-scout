## Housing

Nine selections address keeping housing, move-in costs, and shelter access for families, single men, single women, survivors, pregnant women and veteran households. Separate intake programs are retained even when operated by the same organization. A coordinated-entry record is not counted as an additional supply of beds.

Remaining gaps: No immediately open general affordable-housing waitlist is established. Youth-only housing, recovery housing and additional rent providers remain in the reserve. ARM's closed waitlist should be revisited later. Shelter capacity, referral routes and funding must be checked; these selections are useful curation targets, not guaranteed placements.

## ID Recovery

Nine selections distinguish agencies that issue documents from local organizations that help clients obtain them. Three local assistance routes provide choices when evidence, fees, mailing address or schedules are the obstacle. National immigration and Social Security routes remain represented.

Remaining gaps: Out-of-state birth certificates, military discharge records and corrections-specific release documents remain reserve pathways. Source records still need clearer descriptions of fees and alternative identity evidence. Tribal credentials apply only to that tribe's members.

### 1. Homeless ID Project — identification and document storage

Check before curating: Confirm the Mesa satellite schedule and holiday changes. The issuing agency still controls the document and timing.

### 2. Paz de Cristo — ID vouchers, birth certificates and mail

- [Matthew's Crossing](https://matthewscrossing.org/): The official site describes school-year weekend backpacks and campus food closets. The current Mesa school roster and referral process still need confirmation.
- [La Mesa Resource Center](https://lamesaministries.org/resource-center/): The official page describes personal help with identification documents, applications, and mail. Fee funding and current bus-pass stock remain separate questions.
- [City of Mesa housing](https://www.mesaaz.gov/Resident-Resources/Housing): The city identifies its security-deposit assistance program. This supports the pathway, not a guarantee of an open application window or funds.
- [Valley Metro RideChoice](https://www.valleymetro.org/ridechoice): Direct page access was blocked by the research tool. This is not evidence of closure. Retain the reviewed program distinctions and require confirmation of Mesa-specific eligibility before curation.
