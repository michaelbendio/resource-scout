## Approved resource text

**Express Employment Professionals · Mesa Staffing Office**

Helps people find temporary, part-time, and full-time jobs with employers in the Mesa area.

**Phone:** 480-820-3700  
**Address:** 849 N. Dobson Rd., Suite 107, Mesa, AZ 85201  
**Email:** jobs.mesaaz@expresspros.com  
**Hours:** Monday–Thursday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; Friday, 8 a.m.–3 p.m.  
**Website:** https://www.expresspros.com/us-arizona-mesa/job-seekers/job-seekers

**Programs and Services**

One application can connect you with several job opportunities. Work includes warehouse and manufacturing jobs, skilled trades, office work, customer service, and professional positions. Some temporary jobs may lead to permanent employment.

Express also offers job-search support, including résumé help, interview tips, and free skills training. There is no job placement fee.

**Eligibility Requirements**

Requirements vary by job. Placement depends on your skills, availability, and the employer’s requirements.

Ask what documents, experience, or licenses you need for the jobs that interest you. The Mesa website does not provide one document list or age requirement for all applicants.

**How to Best Connect**

Call the Mesa office or search and apply through its website.

When calling, explain what kind of work you want and when you can work. Ask how to apply and whether you need an appointment for in-person help.

After applying, you may complete an interview and skills assessment. Placement depends on a suitable job being available.

**Access**

Serves job seekers seeking work with employers served by the Mesa office. Job locations vary; check where you would report to work before accepting an assignment.

**Important Information to Know**

- Openings change often. Check that a job is still available.
- Ask about pay, work hours, and any testing, equipment, or transportation costs.
- Benefits vary. Ask what benefits are available and how long you must work to qualify.
