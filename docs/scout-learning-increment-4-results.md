# Increment 4: the feedback loop and a fresh learning test

September 9, 2026 · v2.0

**Increment 4 is implemented. The new Employment test showed useful changes, but mixed overall results. The proposed lesson remains evaluated and inactive.** No office resources or delivered Mesa files were changed.

## What Scout can now do

- Bring package edits and answered curator questions into a feedback queue. It identifies resolved, reopened, changed and removed questions, preserves conflicting evidence, and can flag a reappearing resource for comparison with an earlier editorial exclusion.
- Give that evidence to an editor to propose a small reusable method. Resource facts, policy preferences and learning hypotheses have separate destinations. Scout stores the editor's reasoning, examples and counterevidence; it does not turn every edit into a new rule.
- Activate a lesson after a successful applicable experiment and an explicit recorded review. Activation is limited to its tested office, category and research/editorial stage. Conflicting amendments require a reviewed replacement.
- Roll back active guidance. Earlier research and editor assignments retain their exact original instructions. A replacement experiment compares with the amendment currently in use.

This runs through Scout's operator commands. Curators continue using their existing resolution notes and resource packages; they do not have another learning form to fill out. An AI editor still has to interpret the evidence. There is no unattended background learning service.

## The new test

Two fresh **Astra** sessions researched the same eight Denver Employment leads. Both used the unchanged Employment playbook, identical neutral instructions and equal budgets; one also received the proposed first-reachable-help lesson. Neither received the other result, edited Mesa summaries or the evaluation rubric.

| Difference worth discussing | Existing guidance | With the proposed lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Step Denver | Kept an explicitly residential recovery/employment option. | **Excluded it as stand-alone public job help**, while preserving recovery as a possible alternative. |
| New Heights | Reserved the targeted employment referral. | **Kept the targeted CDOC referral and found career help available while waitlisted.** |
| Essential details | Included Step's published fees and outside-financial-support restriction. | **Omitted those details from the recovery alternative** and asked a partly source-answerable fee question. |
| Remaining six leads | Similar practical selections and limitations. | No decisive selection improvement; some clearer entry-route descriptions. |

The [New Heights page](https://www.flydenver.com/business-and-community/ceea/career-pathways/new-heights-pilot-program/) supports the waitlist-help finding. The [Step application page](https://stepdenver.org/apply/) supplies the missing fees and financial-support restriction. The assessment does **not** treat a different retain/reserve/exclude label as proof that one researcher was wrong. The Step completeness concern applies to the recovery alternative still offered, not to a hypothetical employment handout for an excluded entry.

The baseline produced 22 question entries; the candidate produced 21. Most were consequential. Both also asked partly source-answerable questions. Counting questions alone would miss the important differences.

My judgment is **no clear overall benefit yet**, with a useful positive signal. This is not a verdict that the principle is bad. One small pair, live-source variation, visible arm labels and my own unblinded assessment cannot establish reliable causation. Under the predeclared rule, mixed results did not trigger a confirmation trial or activation.

## Time and instrumentation

- Existing guidance: **3 minutes 38 seconds**; candidate: **4 minutes 2 seconds**.
- Both completed eight leads and made 13 web tool calls, without intervention.
- Both had a 20-minute process ceiling. Page allowances were instructed; the CLI trace does not expose complete batched page counts, so those counts are not independently verified.
- Token usage and cache counts are saved. Dollar cost is **unknown under the subscription**, not zero. Requested model/settings were `gpt-6-astra`, high effort; the CLI did not independently report backend model identity.

These are bounded lead-research times, not estimates for a complete category or office. One pair does not justify another speed optimization. The next measurement priority is source-page accounting and avoiding questions already answered by the reviewed sources.
