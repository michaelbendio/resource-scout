# Kinds checklist from Provo, tried on Welfare Square — 30 September 2026

Claude, at Michael's request (idea 1 of
[populating-a-new-office-ideas-20260930.md](populating-a-new-office-ideas-20260930.md)).
Read-only: Provo's curated directory from its verified backup of 26 September, and
Welfare Square's prepared-resources file of 30 September. Nothing was imported or changed.

## Method

1. Read all 191 of Provo's curated resources (419 category memberships) and wrote down the
   *kinds* of help they are instances of, per Scout category, each with a Provo example:
   **134 kinds in 21 categories**, plus two for every office (211, an online directory).
   The checklist is [`list-seeding-trial-claude-20260930/kinds-checklist-from-provo.json`](list-seeding-trial-claude-20260930/kinds-checklist-from-provo.json).
2. Judged, by reading, which kinds Welfare Square's 209 starter-set entries cover.
3. For notable missing kinds, searched the whole Welfare Square file (1,085 resources) to
   see whether Scout had the resource in the reserve but did not choose it, or had it not
   at all.

## What stands out

1. **The Church's own resources are missing, in the office named after them.** Welfare
   Square is the Church's welfare complex, yet Scout's file has no bishops' storehouse at
   all, no Deseret Industries for clothing or household goods (only DI job training), and no
   Church employment centre. Church education (BYU Pathway, Ensign College, EnglishConnect,
   the self-reliance course) is in the reserve but not chosen. Provo's curators put the
   storehouse, DI and Church employment services first in their categories. Idea 3 (ask the
   Church's own people first) would have caught every one; the checklist would have flagged
   them all as missing.
2. **Housing's starters repeat Homeless Services.** Five of Housing's ten starters are
   shelters also in Homeless Services, so the kinds of longer-term housing help (transitional
   housing, repairs, homebuying, shared housing, listings) are absent. A checklist by kind
   stops that: a shelter satisfies a Homeless Services kind, not a Housing one.
3. **Some kinds were found but not chosen.** NAMI, Habitat for Humanity, the Rape Recovery
   Center, Easterseals-Goodwill SCSEP, the Utah Independent Living Center and BYU Pathway are
   all in the reserve. The checklist would promote them into the starters.
4. **A few kinds were not found at all**: the storehouse, Help Me Grow, The INN Between, a
   tobacco quitline, the Church employment centre.
5. **Welfare Square adds kinds Provo lacks**, and the checklist should grow from them:
   prenatal Medicaid, home visiting, child-care assistance, a crisis nursery, a maternal
   mental-health line, address confidentiality and victim compensation for survivors,
   walk-in youth crisis care, birth-certificate fee waivers and consular ID, Medicaid ride
   benefits, water-bill help. Each office's curation improves the list for the next.

## What it suggests for Scout

- **Give Scout the checklist**, and have it find the local instance of each kind, category
  by category, before anything else. Starters are then chosen to cover the kinds; the reserve
  is whatever is left.
- **Put the Church's own resources in the checklist explicitly** (bishops' storehouse,
  Deseret Industries, the employment centre, Family Services, Church education), and find
  them from the Church's own locators rather than by web search.
- **Keep one checklist for all offices**, and add each office's genuinely new kinds after
  its curation, as Welfare Square's would.

## Limits

- The judging is Claude's, by reading names and one-line contributions; some calls are
  close (for example whether a county home-visiting programme covers "parent support").
- Provo's directory is one office's choice, not a standard, and a Utah one; Mesa or Las
  Vegas would need the Arizona and Nevada versions of the state-level kinds (idea 2).
