# Ogden: what is missing

From the LSScout run of 6 October 2026, which found and wrote up the 140 resources now in
WSRS-TSO's Ogden office (unreviewed). Each category's lists were read, a direct search was
made where they ran out, and nothing was kept without a page read. This is what the run
**could not find on a readable page**. Someone who knows Ogden may know these; anything
found should be added through the app.

The full notes, category by category, are the `thin` fields in
`~/scout-claude/lsscout-ogden-20261006/categories/*.json`, and `gapsAfterLsscout` in
`ogden.json`.

## Details to confirm while curating

- **Workforce Services' Ogden phone** differs between lists: 801-526-9675 on the county
  flyer, 866-435-7414 on 211.
- **Griffin Memorial's food pantry** phone differs between lists; the write-up uses 801-399-2171.
- **Lantern House's** own site shows no hours or intake times; what is written rests on
  its 211 page.
- **Church Employment Services** was left out: 211 gives it two different addresses and
  the run could not settle which is current. Worth adding once confirmed.
- **North View Senior Center:** its site says 55 and over, 211 says 60.
- **St. Martha's Baby Project** takes mothers referred by hospitals and WIC; its phone is
  801-428-1294 on the programme page and 801-394-5944 on 211.
- **Adult education** details come from the State directory; the school districts' sites
  could not be read.
- **Left out on purpose:** Adult Probation and Parole's Ogden office (it supervises
  people rather than taking applications) and Lantern House's birth-certificate fee help
  as a separate entry (it is part of Lantern House's write-up).
