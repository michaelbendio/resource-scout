# Mesa — starters and five more

Read-only evaluation. The first five alternatives add a Type absent from the starter set first, then sort alphabetically; this is a preview, not a stored rank. WSRS-TSO will use actual curated Type coverage. No resource is marked Curated here. Items needing resolution stay in the administrator list.

## Housing

Nine selections address keeping housing, move-in costs, and shelter access for families, single men, single women, survivors, pregnant women and veteran households. Separate intake programs are retained even when operated by the same organization. A coordinated-entry record is not counted as an additional supply of beds.

### Starter set

- **A New Leaf - financial assistance, housing and utilities** — Starts with Mesa's own rent-assistance intake, helping eligible households preserve housing before shelter becomes necessary. Limitation: City of Mesa eligibility and limited appointment releases apply. Confirm the rent-intake schedule and funds; utility intake uses a different route.

- **Maricopa County Human Services / Community Legal Services · Legal Assistance for Renters (eviction for nonpayment)** — Adds legal screening and possible representation for nonpayment eviction, a different intervention from paying rent or finding a bed. Limitation: Case eligibility and acceptance need screening. Confirm current intake and any deadlines directly; assistance is not guaranteed.

- **Family Housing Hub — family coordinated entry** — Provides a coordinated shelter-access route for families with minor children, including a possible Mesa intake option. Limitation: Assessment does not guarantee a bed. Saved addresses and satellite schedules conflict; confirm the current intake route before travel.

- **A New Leaf - homeless services and housing** — Adds a Mesa residential homelessness program for adult men, who do not fit the family-with-children pathway. Limitation: Prescreening and program rules apply. Confirm the destination; A New Leaf's administrative address is not the program's intake address.

- **UMOM New Day Centers** — Adds an explicit shelter pathway for single women, complementing the family Hub and men's program. Limitation: The saved record combines several UMOM programs. Confirm the single-women intake route and waiting list rather than assuming the family Hub handles every case.

- **Sojourner Center · Emergency/transitional DV housing and Pet Companion Shelter** — Adds confidential survivor housing and a distinct pet-support pathway, an important constraint that ordinary shelter listings may not address. Limitation: Phoenix-based; housing and pet placement have separate availability. Confirm intake and pet arrangements without publishing a confidential shelter location.

- **City of Mesa Housing Authority · Security and Utility Deposit Assistance Program** — Addresses the up-front cost of entering an eligible rental, which differs from rent-arrears assistance and shelter placement. Limitation: Confirm the application window, funding and timing before lease signing or occupancy. Landlord, inspection and income rules apply.

- **National Community Health Partners · Housing for Heroes (SSVF)** — Adds a separate provider and veteran-specific prevention or rehousing assistance for qualifying households. Limitation: SSVF eligibility, current intake and available funds must be screened; this is not a general rent fund.

- **Maggie's Place · Maternity and parenting housing / Family Success Center** — Adds housing tailored to homeless pregnant adults, with parenting support beyond an ordinary emergency-shelter listing. Limitation: Program-specific safety and household rules can exclude applicants with other children in their care. Confirm eligibility and openings; home addresses can be confidential.

Gaps: No immediately open general affordable-housing waitlist is established. Youth-only housing, recovery housing and additional rent providers remain in the reserve. ARM's closed waitlist should be revisited later. Shelter capacity, referral routes and funding must be checked; these selections are useful curation targets, not guaranteed placements.
