# Welfare Square — starters and five more

Read-only evaluation. The first five alternatives add a Type absent from the starter set first, then sort alphabetically; this is a preview, not a stored rank. WSRS-TSO will use actual curated Type coverage. No resource is marked Curated here. Items needing resolution stay in the administrator list.

## Addiction

These 10 cover front-door assessment, county/provider access, broad continuum care, opioid medication treatment, urgent assessment, gender-specific detox, women/mothers, pregnancy/postpartum care and harm reduction. After them, the largest gaps were medical/inpatient detox, ambulatory detox, additional MOUD options, culturally specific care, youth, Veterans, telehealth, peer/family support, recovery housing, homeless/justice routes and alternative private continuums.

### Starter set

- **Huntsman Mental Health Institute Assessment & Referral Services (ARS)** — Strong neutral front door for SUD assessment and referral without pretending ARS itself is treatment. Limitation: Placement and availability are not guaranteed.

- **Valley Behavioral Health Substance Use Services Access** — Walk-in/provider access point for outpatient and residential Valley services, especially useful when a client needs assessed level-of-care placement. Limitation: Openings, payer and residential fit require Valley Access confirmation.

- **Odyssey House of Utah Substance Use Treatment Continuum** — Broad nonprofit continuum with adult, youth, parent-with-children, justice-involved, outpatient, residential and related recovery pathways through one admissions route. Limitation: Placement is assessment-dependent; do not send clients to residential addresses.

- **Project Reality Opioid Treatment and Integrated Care** — Strong opioid-treatment/MOUD option with low-income or uninsured relevance. Limitation: Medication hours and site-specific details still need exact confirmation.

- **Intermountain Behavioral Health Access Center at LDS Hospital** — No-appointment urgent behavioral health and addiction/withdrawal assessment route when routine outpatient referral is too slow or acuity is unclear. Limitation: Hospital billing, admission decisions and 988/911/ER appropriateness must be considered.

- **Volunteers of America, Utah Men's Detoxification at Recovery on Redwood** — Direct 24/7 men's social-model detox, with case management, peer support, MAT access and treatment linkage. Limitation: Non-medical detox; call first for medical fit and bed availability.

- **Volunteers of America, Utah Center for Women and Children Detoxification Program** — Direct women/parenting-women social detox counterpart to the men's program, including possible young-child accommodation. Limitation: Current location must be confirmed before travel because official sources conflict.

- **House of Hope Salt Lake City Treatment Programs** — Women and mothers with children receive residential/outpatient/day-treatment pathways that the general continuums do not replace. Limitation: Children's services are tied to mothers in treatment and are not stand-alone child care.

- **SUPeRAD Clinic - Substance Use and Pregnancy Care** — Direct no-referral pregnancy/postpartum SUD care, harm-reduction support and medication coordination. Limitation: Not an emergency line; medication and location details need intake.

- **Utah Harm Reduction Coalition Harm Reduction and Treatment Access** — Direct harm-reduction supplies, naloxone, syringe exchange and treatment access phone route. Limitation: Schedules, supplies and mobile locations can change.

Gaps: Same-day detox beds, payer acceptance and clinical admission eligibility cannot be established from directory research; clients need direct assessment and current intake confirmation.

## Disability

Ten starters cover the strongest broad front doors before narrower alternatives: work, federal disability benefits, Medicaid/LTC access, DSPD HCBS, sensory services, transportation, assistive technology, legal rights, and family/youth navigation.

### Starter set

- **Utah State Office of Rehabilitation Vocational Rehabilitation** — Core public employment front door for people with disabilities who need assessment, counseling, job services, training or accommodations to work. Limitation: Eligibility and service planning are counselor-determined, and timing can depend on order of selection or plan development.

- **Social Security SSI and SSDI Benefits Application** — Federal SSI/SSDI application path for cash and disability benefits, useful across diagnoses and ages once work-limiting disability is at issue. Limitation: SSA decides eligibility and timelines can be long; legal or navigation help may be needed for appeals.

- **Medicaid and Long-Term Care Medicaid Application** — Statewide Medicaid and long-term-care Medicaid application front door, needed before many disability, waiver, in-home and medical supports can start. Limitation: Broad application pathway, not a disability service by itself; DWS determines the actual Medicaid eligibility category.

- **Community Supports Waiver** — DSPD Community Supports Waiver gives the starter set a major home- and community-based-services path for people with intellectual disabilities or related conditions. Limitation: Waitlist, Medicaid and ICF/ID level-of-care criteria make this important but not immediate.

- **Utah State Office of Rehabilitation Division of Services for the Blind and Visually Impaired** — State blind/low-vision rehabilitation front door for employment, independent living, low vision and assistive aids. Limitation: Documentation and program criteria vary; vocational components may route through VR.

- **Utah State Office of Rehabilitation Division of Services of the Deaf and Hard of Hearing - Sanderson Community Center** — Major statewide Deaf, hard-of-hearing and DeafBlind community/service front door through USOR and Sanderson Community Center. Limitation: Classes, events and appointments may require scheduling or registration.

- **Utah Transit Authority ADA Paratransit Eligibility and Service** — Direct ADA paratransit eligibility and ride-service path for riders who cannot use fixed-route transit because of disability. Limitation: Requires eligibility evaluation and has service-area limits; it is not same-day taxi service.

- **Utah Assistive Technology Program / Utah Assistive Technology Foundation** — Broad assistive-technology access and affordability front door covering loans, financing, grants/refurbished devices and the Salt Lake lab connection. Limitation: Each funding or device route has separate approval and availability rules.

- **Disability Law Center Protection and Advocacy Legal Help** — Protection-and-advocacy legal rights resource for Utahns with disability-related discrimination, rights or service-access problems. Limitation: Resource limits and priorities mean not every inquiry receives representation.

- **Utah Parent Center Disability Family Support and Transition Education** — Family and youth disability support, transition education and DSPD/school navigation; final bundle should broaden the name beyond Transition University. Limitation: Education and navigation, not legal representation, clinical care or guaranteed DSPD services.

Gaps: Remaining gaps after starters include additional waiver populations, work incentives, legal/ombuds alternatives, brain/spinal injury navigation, equipment/supplies, child sensory and early-intervention routes, adult day/caregiver/home supports, home modification, adaptive recreation, condition-specific supports and provider alternatives.
