# Mesa — starters and five more

Read-only evaluation. The first five alternatives add a Type absent from the starter set first, then sort alphabetically; this is a preview, not a stored rank. WSRS-TSO will use actual curated Type coverage. No resource is marked Curated here. Items needing resolution stay in the administrator list.

## Housing

Ten complementary usable starters spanning distinct pathways: statewide navigation (211), the single-adult and family coordinated-entry front doors, Mesa's shelter for families/women/seniors, Mesa's men's shelter, Mesa's rent/utility crisis aid, the rental-subsidy rehousing route, the East Valley DV shelter, the 12-17 youth crisis line, and the veterans' walk-in access point. The set deliberately mixes service types - navigation, entry/assessment, shelter, financial aid, rehousing, safety, youth, veterans - rather than several variants of one service, and each limitation is stated so missionaries can set expectations before calling. All needs-resolution records (Helaman House, SAFEDVS, the tracker, UMOM youth crisis/ILP, NREC, Dismas, the Resource Village, Mesa I-HELP) remain outside the starter set with administrator-focused reasons, and reserve usable resources (deposit help, the Mesa Resource Line, second DV shelters, Vista Colina, HUD-VASH, AHP/H2O, DDD lanes, additional shelter capacity) carry considerations written against this actual set. No tiers or reserve ranks are introduced.

### Starter set

- **211 Arizona Helpline (2-1-1)** — Statewide one-number front door: live specialists 9 a.m.-7 p.m. daily, a Housing Crisis Hotline, and shelter/housing/rent/utility referrals with Spanish-language support. Limitation: Referral-only - it provides no beds or funds and may not know same-day shelter availability.

- **Brian Garcia Welcome Center – single adult coordinated entry (Keys to Change)** — The single-adult coordinated entry front door - 24/7 walk-in assessment, diversion and shelter/housing referrals for adults across Maricopa County. Limitation: Central Phoenix walk-in; no placement guarantee; phone variants in older listings (use 602-883-4509 / 602-229-5155); Spanish-language service not confirmed.

- **Family Housing Hub — family coordinated entry and shelter referrals (UMOM New Day Centers)** — The countywide family coordinated entry and shelter waitlist - the front door for families with children seeking shelter and housing programs. Limitation: Long waits (200+ families, about three months reported) and inconsistent posted hours - call before visiting.

- **City of Mesa - Office of Homeless Solutions · Off the Streets emergency shelter (Windermere Hotel site)** — Mesa's main emergency shelter for families, women, seniors and DV survivors - hotel-based, 65 rooms/111 beds, with meals, 24/7 staffing, case management and housing plans. Limitation: Referral-only (no walk-ups) via 480-644-HOPE; about a six-week wait reported and the operator transition to Mercy House - confirm current status.

- **A New Leaf · East Valley Men's Center (EVMC)** — Mesa's 104-bed men's emergency shelter with a defined intake path (online pre-screen plus the (877) 211-8661 placement hotline). Limitation: No phone intakes; beds via waitlist and availability can change daily; children under 18 are not allowed on site.

- **A New Leaf · MesaCAN Rent and Utility Assistance** — Mesa's rent/utility crisis window with a Spanish application path, documented criteria and a clear document list. Limitation: Mesa residents only; first-come funding with narrow appointment windows (first-Thursday rent line, limited slots).

- **A New Leaf · Rapid ReHousing (rental subsidy and case management)** — The rental-subsidy rehousing route (up to 12 months plus move-in help and case management) that shows clients what coordinated entry can lead to beyond shelter. Limitation: No direct applications - entry only through coordinated entry (211); funding and timing vary.

- **A New Leaf · Autumn House Domestic Violence Shelter (East Valley/Mesa)** — The East Valley's local DV shelter - 22 beds, any gender, pet-friendly, screened through A New Leaf's 24-hour hotline. Limitation: Confidential location; placement not guaranteed; the May 2026 countywide DV hotline transition means confirming the current intake route.

- **Safe Place — Youth Crisis Entry (Ages 12–17), Maricopa County** — The 12-17 crisis entry - call, text or walk into Safe Place sites (QuikTrip, Valley Metro, libraries, fire stations) for immediate youth help countywide. Limitation: Overnight shelter not guaranteed; use the current 44357 text instructions (older guides show 69866).

- **VA Community Resource & Referral Center (CRRC) – Phoenix (veteran housing and coordinated entry)** — The veterans' walk-in front door - housing assessments and referrals (transitional, permanent, eviction prevention), the HUD-VASH pathway, benefits and health care, free of charge. Limitation: Weekdays 7:30 a.m.-4 p.m.; central Phoenix; referrals depend on capacity - arrive early for same-day referrals.

Gaps: No confirmed emergency shelter capacity for young adults 18-24 in Mesa/East Valley: the UMOM youth records are unconfirmed or historical (operator transferred to Child Crisis Arizona), Covenant House has no Arizona site, and only Safe Place (12-17) plus outreach/coordinated-entry points (NAC; UMOM youth outreach) are verified.; No general hotel/motel voucher program and no safe-parking pathway: Red Cross assistance is disaster-triggered, UMOM heat relief is seasonal and referral-based, and hotel-based shelters (Off the Streets, Sue's Espacio, Rio Fresco) are referral-only.; Reentry housing: no confirmed community-referral reentry beds in Phoenix (Dismas' Phoenix facility unconfirmed); H2O and HUD-VASH touch reentry but require health-plan or VA routes.; No open voucher waitlists captured: Mesa HCV, HAMC, Tempe and Phoenix lists are closed or referral-only - no immediate Section 8 application pathway.; Mesa I-HELP rotating shelter unresolved - no confirmed rotating-shelter intake for Mesa adults (operator largely resolved to LSS-SW serving adult women, current operation unconfirmed).; Eviction/tenant legal defense is not represented as a resource (AZEvictionHelp.org appears only as a referral mention).; East Valley single-women capacity: no confirmed Mesa-area beds for women without children; Halle Women's Center (Phoenix) and DV shelters are the closest options.; No confirmed women-veterans-specific beds in Maricopa County (MANA House described for male veterans; Ashley's Place within U.S.VETS noted but not separately verified).
